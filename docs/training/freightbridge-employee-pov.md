# FreightBridge Employee POV

Training Mode is framed as a workplace simulation. The learner is a new FreightBridge Integration Support Analyst, not an omniscient observer of every partner system.

## Learner Role

The learner works for FreightBridge. Their job is to investigate partner integration issues using evidence FreightBridge can realistically access, explain what happened, and verify that the flow recovered.

## What FreightBridge Can Observe

- Inbound HTTP requests received by FreightBridge.
- Safe JSON payloads received by FreightBridge.
- HTTP status responses returned by FreightBridge.
- Partner configuration stored by FreightBridge.
- X12 documents generated by FreightBridge.
- SFTP upload/poll activity visible to FreightBridge.
- X12 `997`, `990`, and `214` documents received by FreightBridge.
- Transaction, processing-log, and integration-error records created by FreightBridge.
- Mapping profile metadata used by FreightBridge.
- Support messages from Apex or Midwest contacts.

## What FreightBridge Cannot Observe

- Apex internal server logs.
- Midwest private databases or private TMS screens.
- Partner implementation details that were not communicated to FreightBridge.
- Magical statements about what happened inside a partner system.

If partner-side context is needed, Training Mode presents it as a partner message such as "Apex Integration Support says..." or "Midwest EDI Support says..." rather than direct internal visibility.

## Communication Model

- Mike, Integration Manager, gives briefings, incident updates, questions, and debriefs.
- Apex and Midwest messages are synthetic support communications from external trading partners.
- Training Mode does not integrate with real email, chat, or ticketing systems.

## Mission Progression

- Orientation: healthy integration from FreightBridge evidence.
- Beginner: inbound authentication/parsing problems.
- Intermediate: validation, duplicate, and business-rule failures.
- Advanced: X12, callback, and SFTP issues.
- Final Shift: end-to-end production-style incident simulation.

Only Mission 1 is implemented in Milestone 24. Later missions remain locked or coming soon.

## Training Mode vs Advanced Console

Training Mode is a guided learning shell with mission briefings, evidence, hints, notes, and debriefs. Advanced Console is the real FreightBridge analyst surface for dashboards, transactions, failures, business trace, Integration Lab, partners, and mappings.

Training Mode never exposes bearer tokens, SFTP credentials, database URLs, private keys, or Supabase secrets.
